# Directory

The Directory is a table with every position and every person in the organization. Use it to search, filter and compare without walking the tree.

## What each row shows

- **A position**: with the name of its current holder, or **Vacant** if it has none. If the position has a current replacement, it appears in the **Replacement** column.
- **A person with no current position**: with "No position" in the Job title column.

A person with two positions appears in two rows. Someone who is only a replacement appears in the row of the position they replace.

After **Replacement** there is one column for each unit type you use (for example Division, Area, Subarea): it shows the nearest unit of that type going up, including the position's own unit. The person's email, contract type, site and schedule follow.

## Search and filter

- The search box looks in the names of the person and the replacement, the job title and the holder's email, ignoring case and accents ("munoz" finds "Muñoz").
- **Unit** includes its whole branch. **View in the directory**, on the People tab of a unit's panel, opens the directory with just that filter. **Job title**, **contract type**, **site** and **schedule** filter by exact value (deactivated ones too); contract type, site and schedule are the holder's, so a vacancy never matches them. **Tag** uses the tags of the position's unit. **Status** separates filled positions, vacancies and people with no position.
- **Clear filters** removes them all. Filters and sort order are remembered on this computer.

## Sort and open

Press the **Person**, **Job title** or first unit column header (it sorts by the full unit path) to sort; press again to reverse. Empty cells always stay at the end.

Press a row (or Tab to the name and Enter) to open the person's record; on a vacancy, the position opens.
