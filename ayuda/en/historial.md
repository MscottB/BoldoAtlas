# Change history

::: info In short
**History** shows every change you made, from newest to oldest. From there you can undo or redo a single change, or go back to an earlier point.
:::

Open **History** in the side menu, right below **Review**. Changes are grouped by day. Each one shows the time, what you did ("You renamed Sales to North") and how many items it touched. The list loads in parts as you scroll down.

- The **You are here** line marks how far what is done goes.
- What is undone looks faded and is marked **Undone**, also an old change you undid on its own.

## View a change

Click a change to open it. You will see each item it touched and, field by field, how it was before and how it ended up. Below there are three buttons:

- **Go to**: opens the unit or position in the tree, or the person's record. It does not appear if that no longer exists.
- **Undo this action**, or **Redo this action** if it is undone.
- **Go back to this point**.

## Undo or redo a single change

Click **Undo this action** to undo only that change, even an old one. If a later change touched the same thing, one cannot be undone without the other: a notice names those changes and offers **Undo the N together**, which undoes them all in one step. **Cancel** changes nothing.

**Redo this action** works the same way with an undone change, and offers **Redo the N together** when needed.

Some changes cannot be undone, and say so below their sentence:

- a **permanent deletion** of a person;
- a change that touched a person who was later permanently deleted, because their data is gone.

## Go back to a point

**Go back to this point** undoes, in one step, every change made after the one you chose. It tells you how many first. The undone changes stay in the list and you can redo them from here. It only appears on a change that is done.

`Ctrl` + `Z` and `Ctrl` + `Y` still undo and redo the last change, in any view. More in [Keyboard shortcuts](atajos.md).

## Search and filter

- **Search by name** shows the changes that touched something with that name, for example "Sales". It ignores case and accents.
- **From** and **To** limit the list to those days.

In the record of a unit, a position or a person, **View history** opens this view with only the changes to that item. **Show all** removes that filter.

## Export to Excel

**Export this list to Excel**, at the top right, saves every change that matches the search and filters: the date and time, the sentence, whether it is done or undone and how many items it touched. Before saving you must accept a notice, because the file is not encrypted; the details are in [Export to Excel](directorio.md#export-to-excel).

## How long it is kept

Changes are kept for the period you choose in **Settings › Change history** (1 year unless you change it). The oldest ones are deleted when the organization opens. Backups carry the history; `.boldo` files do not.

If you view the org chart at a date, or view another organization side by side, you can look at and open the history, but the buttons do not appear.
