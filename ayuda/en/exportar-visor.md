# Export as an HTML viewer

::: info In short
Save the org chart as an **.html** file that opens with a double click in any browser, with nothing to install and no internet. Whoever receives it can fold branches, search for a unit or a person, see it as a list and open the details of each unit.
:::

## Where it opens

It is one more format in the **Export as image** window: choose **HTML viewer** under **Format**. The window opens from the same places as the image: the **Org chart image** card in **Import/Export**, the **Export as image** button in the tree toolbar or right-click on a unit. **Which part**, the **Level of detail** and **Open every branch in the scope** work as they do for the image, and the preview shows what the viewer draws when it opens.

## What each level includes

- **Structure**: the units and, in the details of each one, its path and the titles of its positions. No names, emails or phone numbers of people. It is the one to use when sharing outside.
- **Presentation**: also the name of whoever holds each position, the vacancies and the substitutes.
- **As on screen**: the same as Presentation, drawn with your Customize settings: colors, legend and, if **Show positions** is on, the positions in the drawing.

Notes, photos, costs and descriptions never go in the file.

## Email and phone

With **HTML viewer**, two checkboxes appear under **Contact details**: **Email** and **Phone**. They start unchecked. If you check one, the details of each unit show that information for whoever holds each position, as a link to write to them or call them. In **Structure** they are turned off: that level has no personal data. BoldoAtlas remembers what you checked.

## How to use the file

- Drag to move around, use the mouse wheel to zoom and **Fit to screen** to see it whole again.
- The button on each card folds or unfolds its branch, and the drawing rearranges itself.
- **Search unit, person or position** finds matches regardless of accents or capitals. When you pick a result, the branches open down to that unit and its details open.
- **List** shows the org chart as a text tree that you move through with the arrow keys: up and down to move, right and left to open and close branches, **Enter** to see the details.
- A click on a unit opens its details: the path from the root, the head and the people in its positions.

## It is a snapshot

The viewer shows the org chart as it was when you saved it, with the date and time in its toolbar. It does not update itself: to have today's data, save a new one. If you are viewing the org chart on a past date, the viewer shows that day.

## It asks nothing of the network

Everything is inside the file: the data, the program and the styles. It loads nothing from the internet and sends nothing anywhere, and it works the same from a folder, a USB drive or a downloaded email.

## The viewer is not encrypted

Before saving, a notice reminds you that the file is not encrypted: anyone who opens it will see what it contains. Press **I accept, continue** to go on; without accepting, nothing is saved. Whoever makes it is responsible for where it is saved and who it is shared with. When it finishes, **Open folder** shows the file.
