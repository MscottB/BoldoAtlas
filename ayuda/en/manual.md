# Organization and functions manual

::: info In short
A PDF with the org chart and, for each unit, its positions and functions, ready to print or hand over. You choose which part, whether it includes people, the sheet and the orientation, and you save it.
:::

## Where it opens

- **Import/Export**, in the sidebar: the **Organization manual** card. It comes out with the whole organization, or with the part you choose under **Which part**.
- Right-click on a unit: **Manual for this branch**. The window starts with that branch.

## Options

- **Which part**: the whole organization, or one unit with everything under it.
- **Include people**: with the box checked, the manual has the names of those who hold each position. Unchecked, no name appears on any page, not in the head, not in the org chart, not in the file name: good for sharing outside.
- **Sheet**: **Letter**, **Oficio**, **A4**, **A3** or **A2**.
- **Orientation**: **Landscape** or **Portrait**.

BoldoAtlas remembers what you used last time.

## What the manual contains

1. A **cover** with the organization, the part chosen, the date and the data revision.
2. A **contents** page with the page of each unit.
3. The **general org chart**, on one or more sheets. Where the drawing is cut, the card says "Continues on sheet N", and the next sheet says "Continued from sheet M".
4. **One section per unit**, with its drawing, its head and the table of its positions: position title, description, who holds it and whether it is filled or vacant.

## Org chart on a date

If you are viewing the org chart on a past date, the manual comes out with that day, and the window says so. Go back to today to get the one for today.

## The manual is not encrypted

Before saving, a notice reminds you that the PDF is not encrypted: anyone who opens it will see what it contains. Press **I accept, continue** to go on. Whoever generates it is responsible for where it is saved and who it is shared with. If the manual has more than 200 pages, the notice says so with the number.

## Cancel

While the manual is drawn and put together, the footer of the window shows how far it is and a **Cancel** button. Cancelling, or closing the window, stops everything and saves nothing. If the org chart changes while it is being prepared, the manual is not saved: the window says so and you can save it again.
